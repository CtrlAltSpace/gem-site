Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Gem ("the Bot," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you interact with the Bot on Discord.

Gem is developed and operated by the Gem team ("the Team"), which consists of the individuals responsible for developing, maintaining, moderating, and operating the Bot.

By using the Bot, you acknowledge that your information may be processed as described in this policy. Please do not use the Bot if you do not agree with these practices.

2. Information We Collect

When you interact with the Bot, we may collect and process the following information:

  • Messages and prompts: Messages you send to the Bot and relevant conversation context.
  • Discord identifiers: Your Discord username and user ID. Reports and logs are associated with both identifiers, while memories are associated only with your Discord user ID.
  • Server conversation data: Messages and conversation context from Discord servers in which the Bot operates, where applicable to its memory functionality.
  • Usage and security logs: Records of prompts and relevant session information collected for security, safety, troubleshooting, and service improvement.
  • Feedback and reports: Information you voluntarily submit through the Bot's feedback modal, including feedback, bug reports, and safety reports. These submissions are sent to the Team for review and handling.

Please do not submit confidential information or sensitive personal data, such as home addresses, government identification numbers, passwords, or financial details, to the Bot or through its feedback and reporting features.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Generate responses and provide the Bot's functionality.
  • Maintain conversation context and memory between interactions.
  • Detect misuse, investigate security or safety issues, and troubleshoot problems.
  • Evaluate and improve the Bot's performance.
  • Respond to privacy-related questions and data deletion requests.

We do not claim that all collected information is anonymous. Reports and logs are associated with your username and Discord user ID, while user-specific memory is only associated with your Discord user ID.

4. Memory and Conversation Storage

The Bot includes a memory system designed to retain conversation information across interactions. It uses server-specific memory and user-specific memory.

4.1. Server-Specific Memory

  • The Bot may store conversation information from a Discord server in that server's memory.
  • Server-specific memory is available as context only within the same server and is not shared as server memory with other Discord servers.
  • The Bot may use this information to maintain context and continuity within the relevant server.
  • Server-specific memory may contain messages from multiple participants. This does not mean every participant can directly access the underlying stored records.

4.2. User-Specific Memory

  • The Bot may store conversation information in user-specific memory associated with your Discord user ID.
  • This memory may be used across different servers and channels in which you interact with the Bot.
  • User-specific memory is retrieved for an interaction only when the person interacting with the Bot is using the same Discord account associated with that memory.
  • Other users cannot retrieve your user-specific memory through the Bot's normal memory functionality.

This means information from your conversations may be used as context in another place where you interact with the Bot, even if that interaction takes place in a different server.

4.3. Memory Retention and Deletion

Each memory is retained for up to five (5) months from its creation, after which it should be deleted through the memory retention process.

We may also process memory deletion requests submitted through the contact address provided in this policy.

Memory and security logging are separate practices. Deleting a memory does not necessarily mean that a separate security or session log containing related information has also been deleted.

5. Logging and Data Retention

We log prompts and relevant session information for security, safety, and service improvement.

Logs used for service improvement may be associated with your Discord username and user ID. These logs may be retained for up to one (1) year, after which they should be deleted through our retention process.

Retention periods may differ between individual memories, reports, and separate logs because they serve different purposes.

For questions about our data practices or to request deletion of your information, contact us at ctrlaltspace.prod@proton.me .

6. Feedback, Bug Reports, and Safety Reports

The Bot provides an optional command that opens a modal where you can submit feedback, report bugs, or make safety reports. Submitting a report is entirely voluntary.

Any information you enter into the modal is sent to the Team for review and handling. You should only include information that is relevant to your submission.

Feedback, bug reports, and safety reports are associated with your Discord username and user ID. This allows the Team to identify the account associated with a submission and, where appropriate, follow up on or investigate the reported issue.

Please do not include unnecessary personal information, confidential information, passwords, or other sensitive details in a submission.

These submissions are distinct from the Bot's conversation memories and ordinary logging. The reports will be sent to our team's DM and may be retained indefinitelyrequested to be removed.

7. Third-Party AI Provider: Groq

The Bot uses Groq to provide AI-related functionality.

We have enabled Zero Data Retention (ZDR) for Groq under our configured service arrangement. Under this arrangement, Groq does not retain the input and output data covered by ZDR, and Groq does not use your user data to train its models.

Your prompts and relevant conversation context may still be transmitted to Groq for processing and response generation. Groq's processing of this information is separate from our own logging and memory systems.

Therefore, Groq's ZDR configuration does not mean that the Bot itself refrains from storing conversation information or logs.

8. Data Sharing

We do not intentionally make user-specific memory available to other users through the Bot's normal memory functionality.

Information may be transmitted to service providers, including Groq, when necessary to operate the Bot and generate responses.

We may also disclose information where reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law or respond to valid legal requests.

9. Data Security

We take data protection seriously and use the data-handling practices implemented for the Bot to support its operation and security.

However, no online service or method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should avoid submitting confidential information or sensitive personal data to the Bot.

10. Minors

The Bot is not intended for use by minors.

If we become aware that a minor has used the service, we will take steps to delete their associated data and restrict their access to the Bot, subject to applicable technical and legal limitations.

11. Your Privacy Requests

You may contact us to ask questions about our data practices or request deletion of information associated with your Discord account.

To help us locate the relevant information, we may need your Discord user ID and sufficient details to identify the records concerned. Please do not send passwords or other confidential authentication information.

Your continued use of the Bot after an updated policy is published is subject to the revised policy, to the extent permitted by applicable law.
